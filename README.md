# Deep Research

Deep Research is a multi-agent research system built with LangChain and LangGraph.

Please choose your preferred language:

- [English](./README.en.md)
- [简体中文](./README.zh-CN.md)

## Overview

Deep Research decomposes complex questions into research plans, executes web research and code-assisted analysis, and generates structured Markdown reports.
